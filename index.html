Background

Research, Data & Music

My training is in experimental psychology. I design experiments, collect behavioral and EEG data, and analyze the results in R, SPSS, and MATLAB. I have produced and DJed techno and ambient music for eight years, and I co-founded an electronic music collective at UC Santa Barbara. My honors thesis joined the two by testing whether rhythm in music changes memory for the order of events.
